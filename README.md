# Favorite Movie: The Book of Life (2014)

**The Book of Life** is an animated musical fantasy comedy film directed by Jorge R. Gutierrez and produced by Guillermo del Toro.

## Overview
Set against the vibrant backdrop of Day of the Dead (*Día de los Muertos*), the story follows **Manolo**, a young bullfighter torn between fulfilling the expectations of his family and following his heart to play music. When gods La Muerte and Xibalba place a wager on who will win the heart of Maria, Manolo embarks on an epic journey across three fantastical worlds to reunite with his true love.

## Key Highlights & Characters
* **Manolo Sánchez** – A noble musician and bullfighter who refuses to harm animals.
* **María Posada** – An independent, spirited, and strong-willed woman.
* **Joaquín Mondragon** – Manolo's competitive childhood friend and town hero.
* **Stunning Visual Style** – Styled after traditional wooden marionettes with rich, vibrant Mexican folklore aesthetics.
* **Memorable Soundtrack** – Features unique acoustic cover versions of popular pop and rock songs.
